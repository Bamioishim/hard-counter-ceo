WELCOME TO WINNIE ARENA. STAY ON THE ROOF.

THE RULES

Best of three. 60 second rounds. No health bars. Hits raise KNOCKBACK %, and the higher it gets, the further you fly. Leave the roof and you lose the round (RING OUT). Time out? Lower knockback takes it.

Everything costs STAMINA. Spam and you'll be RECOVERING while someone bonks you. Landing hits and parries fills HYPE. At 100% press T for your FINISHER.

THE COUNTERS

LIGHT is fast, but ducks under CROUCH. HEAVY crushes crouchers and late parries. A well-timed PARRY (PERFECT PARRY) staggers anything, then your next hit is a COUNTERPOINT. FEINT baits parries. Full CHARGE (hold E) fires your SIGNATURE MOVE.
